Why Assertions Are Close but Quality Differs Key Insight

Opus 4.6 already knows many MATLAB R2025b APIs correctly. Both configs pass ~97% of assertions. But the blind comparator detected consistent quality gaps.

Bottom line: Skills don't just prevent crashes — they elevate code from "works" to "production-ready" with domain expertise, complete workflows, and subtle gotcha awareness.
